## What's in BITE-OS

- The `BITE-OS` brand, identity, and visual design (wolf logo, color scheme,
  ASCII banners)
- The `glitch-fetch` gacha engine (`~/.config/glitch/bin/glitch-fetch.sh`) —
  pure-bash random fastfetch executor with aspect-aware template matching
- Performance tuning targeted at older hardware (performance > battery life)
- The custom module/template structure under `~/.config/glitch/`
- All branding artwork and ASCII art

If you fork or build on BITE-OS, please keep this file intact and add your
own credits below.

## Third-party rices

themselves are third-party work under their own licences; the BITE-OS

keybind generator, branding and perf tuning) is BITE-OS work.
