On typing things out

Reading code is not the same as writing it. The hand remembers what the eye skips: a bracket, a colon, the exact spelling of a tag.

"You don't know a page until you've typed it."

Three small claims

  1. Typing is slower than reading, and that is the point.
  2. Mistakes are information, not noise.
  3. A page you typed is a page you can change.
